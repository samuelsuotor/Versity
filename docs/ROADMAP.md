# Development Roadmap

## Purpose

This document provides the long-term development roadmap for Versity.

Unlike the Sprint Progress document, which records completed work, this roadmap focuses on the planned evolution of the platform from Version 1 to future releases.

The roadmap is intended to guide development while remaining flexible enough to accommodate improvements and new ideas.

---

# Version 1 — Foundation & Validation

## Goal

Build a polished academic marketplace that validates the business idea through excellent user experience and carefully curated academic content.

Version 1 prioritises simplicity, trust, usability, and quality over feature quantity.

# Version 2 — Business Automation

## Goal

Automate business operations while preserving the successful marketplace built in Version 1.

### Planned Features

- Online payment integration
- Automated payment verification
- Secure downloads
- Order management
- Improved administrator tools
- Business analytics
- Email notifications

Only the administrator dashboard exists throughout Version 2.

---

# Version 3 — Marketplace Expansion

## Goal

Expand Versity into a verified multi-vendor academic marketplace.

### Planned Features

- Student accounts
- Seller dashboards
- Seller verification
- Project submission workflow
- Approval system
- Revenue sharing
- Reviews and ratings

---

# Long-Term Vision

Future versions may introduce:

- Academic software marketplace
- Research resources
- Presentation templates
- Academic notes
- AI-assisted educational tools
- Institutional services

These ideas will only be considered after the objectives of earlier versions have been successfully achieved.

---

# Development Philosophy

Versity follows incremental development.

Rather than implementing every possible feature immediately, the platform evolves through carefully planned versions and disciplined sprint execution.

Every sprint should improve the platform while maintaining consistency, simplicity, and long-term maintainability.
